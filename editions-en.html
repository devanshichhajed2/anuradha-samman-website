The award is presented at a public ceremony each February. Below are the editions held to date.

First edition

2026 · Intellectual disability

21 February 2026, Department of Social Justice campus, Bhopal.

21 February 2026Field · Intellectual disabilityBhopal

Shri Rajendra Baraskar

Special educator · 28 years of continuous work

Shri Rajendra Baraskar has worked with children with intellectual disability for the past 28 years. He gave their teaching and training first priority, and went further — using sport, cultural activity and social participation to bring them into the mainstream. Under his guidance many children have made real gains in confidence and independence.

The high point of the evening was a full performance of the Ramayana by the children of Digdarshika school. At its close the audience rose to its feet, and the hall filled with applause.

Guests of honour: Dr. Ajay Khemaria, Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities, Government of Madhya Pradesh · Shri Ramendra Singh, Secretary, Madhya Pradesh Red Cross Society · Shri Gorelal Barche.

Presented: felicitation with a shawl and shriphal; a citation and a cheque for ₹21,000 from the family of the late Dr. Anuradha.
