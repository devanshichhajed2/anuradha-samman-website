The person remembered

Major Dr. Anuradha

Sensitive, tireless, and given wholly to service — at every stage of her life she chose the work that was difficult and needed doing. Through her warmth and dedication she found a place in everyone’s heart as their “Didi”.

She believed, always, that the particular work special educators do must be recognised by society.

Three stages of a life, and the same dedication in each — the army, then medicine, and finally special education.

Three stages

A life of service

Soldier · Physician · Everyone's "Didi"

First

In the Army

An officer in the Indian Army, awarded Best Officer, and the first woman officer to drive the Arjun tank.

Then

Medicine in tribal Madhya Pradesh

Through some 25 projects she put her medical training to work protecting health in Khargone district and the deeply underserved tribal areas around it — from people affected by leprosy and their families to children with special needs. She brought the same inventiveness to work on the environment and on the dignity of the national flag.

Finally

Given to special education

Recognising how much society needed special education, she gave the last eight to ten years of her life entirely to it — and set before all of us an exceptional example of including children with special needs alongside other children, in the ordinary activities of community life.

This award

Carrying her conviction forward

Holding to that conviction, her family began this award in her memory — for special educators in Madhya Pradesh, in one field of disability each year.
